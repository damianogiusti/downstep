# Changelog

What changes for people who use Downstep. The release takes the section of its version from here,
and the app shows it before and after an update: keep it short, plain English, no technical details.

## 1.3.0

- After an update, Downstep shows what is new.
- Downstep now runs on Intel Macs, with its own download.
- Songs open much faster on Windows, and the app no longer freezes while a song opens.
- Downstep starts a little faster.
- The app asks before it restarts to finish an update.
- Contact: write to me from the app (library menu on Windows, Help menu on Mac).
- Bug fixes and small improvements.

## 1.2.0

- Normalize volume: every song plays at a similar level. Quiet songs are raised, loud ones lowered. Exports keep the original volume.
- If you open Downstep while it is already running, the open window comes to the front.
- Bug fixes and small improvements.
