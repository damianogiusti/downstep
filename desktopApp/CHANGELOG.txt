# Changelog

What changes for people who use Downstep. The release takes the section of its version from here,
and the app shows it before and after an update: keep it short, plain English, no technical details.

## 1.4.0

- Long songs open much faster, and Downstep shows when a song is opening.
- Fine pitch: tune a song in steps of 5 cents, for recordings that are not tuned to A440.
- Fine loop: zoom on the A-B loop to place A and B precisely. On a short loop, B no longer covers A.
- The waveform can be shown under the loop. Turn it on in Settings, from the library menu.
- On Windows, songs imported from YouTube that did not open now work.
- Some YouTube imports refused by the server now work.
- On Windows, Downstep now says when a security policy of the computer blocks it.
- Bug fixes and small improvements.

## 1.3.1

- YouTube links from a playlist or a mix now import the video.
- Some YouTube videos that failed to import now work.
- If a song is too long to open, Downstep now says so.
- Exporting the same backup twice in one day now works on Windows. Backup file names have no spaces.
- Fixed a crash on macOS 14.0 and 14.1.
- On Windows, the next updates no longer stop if an old copy of the app cannot be deleted.
- Bug fixes and small improvements.

## 1.3.0

- After an update, Downstep shows what is new.
- Downstep now runs on Intel Macs, with its own download.
- Songs open much faster on Windows, and the app no longer freezes while a song opens.
- Downstep starts a little faster.
- The app asks before it restarts to finish an update.
- Contact: write to me from the app (library menu on Windows, Help menu on Mac).
- Bug fixes and small improvements.

## 1.2.0

- Normalize volume: every song plays at a similar level. Quiet songs are raised, loud ones lowered. Exports keep the original volume.
- If you open Downstep while it is already running, the open window comes to the front.
- Bug fixes and small improvements.
