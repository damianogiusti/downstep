===================

Downstep is free software under the GNU General Public License v3 (GPL-3.0.txt).
Its complete source code is at https://github.com/damianogiusti/downstep

Downstep uses the third-party components below. The full license texts are in this folder:
GPL-3.0.txt, LGPL-2.0.txt, MPL-2.0.txt, Apache-2.0.txt and FONTS-OFL.txt. The Java runtime keeps
